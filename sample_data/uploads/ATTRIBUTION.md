# Audio sample attribution

`remote-control-design-kickoff.m4a` is a 2 min 15 s excerpt (from 00:49 to 03:04) of meeting
**ES2002a** (Mix-Headset recording) from the **AMI Meeting Corpus**, trimmed and converted to AAC.

- Source: AMI Meeting Corpus, University of Edinburgh — https://groups.inf.ed.ac.uk/ami/corpus/
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0) — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Changes: trimmed to an excerpt; converted from 16 kHz WAV to AAC (.m4a).

It is a real (scenario-based) product-design meeting with four participants, used here to show
voice-to-transcript on natural speech.

`2026-11-05_patient-portal-launch-sync.m4a` is synthetic (macOS text-to-speech), created for this
project. It is short and scripted, so it also produces a decision and action items.
